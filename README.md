# My Favorite Series: Attack on Titan (*Shingeki no Kyojin*)

## Overview
**Attack on Titan** is a Japanese *dark fantasy action* anime series adapted from the manga by Hajime Isayama. Set in a post-apocalyptic world where humanity is forced to live inside cities surrounded by three enormous walls, the story follows **Eren Yeager** as he vows to exterminate the giant humanoid creatures known as *Titans* after they breach his hometown.

> "If you win, you live. If you lose, you die. If you don't fight, you can't win!" — Eren Yeager

---

## Why It Stands Out
* **Unpredictable Storytelling:** Plot twists from Season 1 pay off years later in Season 4 with unmatched foreshadowing.
* **Complex Morality:** There are no simple heroes or villains—every faction has understandable motives.
* **Phenomenal Soundtrack:** Composed by Hiroyuki Sawano, the music elevates every major battle and emotional reveal.
* **Fluid Animation:** Stunning ODM (Omni-Directional Mobility) gear action sequences by Wit Studio and MAPPA.
